## About me

I am a **CTO and Full Stack Developer** with over **7 years of experience** in software development, systems architecture, and technical leadership.

I currently lead the development of **IntegrarPlus**, a custom-built clinical management platform designed to centralize the administrative, clinical, and operational processes of therapeutic clinics.

My work combines:

* end-to-end product development, from planning to production;
* architecture of complex web applications;
* process automation and system integrations;
* security, code quality, and observability;
* infrastructure, deployment, and system operations;
* technical leadership and product decision-making.

I also develop projects for the **Minecraft, Fabric, and Cobblemon** ecosystems, building economy systems, events, land protection, administration tools, and server optimization solutions.

## What I am building

### IntegrarPlus

A clinical management platform built with **Ruby on Rails**, designed around the real operational needs of therapeutic clinics.

Some of its main technical challenges include:

* complex schedules and recurring appointments;
* beneficiary and professional management;
* attendance tracking and reporting;
* documents, contracts, and approval workflows;
* multi-unit support;
* permissions, auditing, and data protection;
* API and artificial intelligence integrations;
* background jobs and document processing;
* automated quality gates using GitHub Actions.

> The source code is private because it contains proprietary business rules and handles sensitive information. Architectural decisions and technical case studies may be shared separately.

### BigBangCraft

An ecosystem of mods, services, and tools for Minecraft servers, focused primarily on **Fabric 1.21.1** and **Cobblemon**.

Some of the systems developed include:

* economy, jobs, and player progression;
* ranks, kits, crates, and VIP benefits;
* land claims and region protection;
* tournaments and events;
* administration and moderation tools;
* entity and performance optimization;
* database and web service integrations.
